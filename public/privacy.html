Privacy Notice

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This notice is a practical website privacy statement based on the information currently supplied by the firm. It is not a legal compliance certification. The firm should have a Nigerian privacy professional review it before launch, particularly its lawful bases, cross-border hosting arrangements and any professional confidentiality duties.

1. Who is responsible

Aibangbee Law Firm (the “Firm”) operates this website in Nigeria. Contact: aibangbeelaw@gmail.com or 0802 329 8459. The address supplied for the Firm is: 25 Airoghionba Street, Off Old Western Boys High School Road, Off 1st East Circular Road, Benin City, Edo State, Nigeria.

2. Information we collect

If you submit a consultation request, we collect your name, email address, telephone number, selected practice area, preferred appointment date and time, and any additional details you choose to provide. The website also processes essential technical information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as server request information and staff authentication session data.

3. Why we use it

We use consultation information to review and respond to enquiries, contact you about a requested appointment, assess whether the Firm may be able to assist, maintain appropriate business and professional records, and protect the website against misuse. Do not submit sensitive case evidence, identity documents, financial details, or confidential facts through this initial website form.

4. Legal basis

Depending on the circumstances, the Firm may process enquiry information to take steps at your request before a possible engagement, to pursue legitimate interests in responding to enquiries and operating the website, to comply with legal or professional obligations, or on the basis of consent where consent is the appropriate legal basis. The applicable basis depends on the specific processing activity and should be documented by the Firm.

5. Cookies and local storage

This website does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. The staff administration area uses an essential, secure session cookie to maintain an authenticated administrator session. A small local-storage preference remembers that the visitor has dismissed this cookie information notice. If analytics, maps, embedded media or other optional services are added later, this notice and the consent controls should be updated before those services are enabled.

6. Storage, hosting and retention

Consultation requests and website content are stored using the Firm’s configured database provider (Neon). The website is intended to be hosted through Vercel. These providers may process or store information in locations outside Nigeria. The Firm should confirm the actual data-region settings, review provider terms and safeguards, and document any international transfer requirements before collecting real client information. Unconverted consultation enquiries are retained for up to 12 months. The website runs a scheduled retention cleanup at startup and approximately hourly, removing consultation requests older than 12 months. The Firm may need to retain particular records longer where required by law, professional obligations, a dispute, or a documented legitimate record-keeping need. This period does not automatically apply to client matter files, which are governed by separate retention requirements.

7. Who may receive information

Access is restricted to authorised Firm personnel. Service providers that host or operate the website and database may process information on the Firm’s behalf. Website images are hosted through Cloudinary when configured. If email notifications are enabled through server environment settings, the consultation contact details may also be processed by the configured transactional email provider. Information may also be disclosed where required by law, a court or competent authority, or where necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. The Firm does not sell consultation information.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law and relevant exceptions, you may request access to personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing, or exercise other rights available under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. You may contact the Firm at aibangbeelaw@gmail.com. The Firm may need to verify your identity and may retain information where a lawful exception applies. You may also raise a concern with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC): ndpc.gov.ng.

9. Security and confidentiality

The Firm uses access controls and technical safeguards intended to protect information. No website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. A website enquiry does not by itself create an attorney-client relationship, and sending information through this site does not guarantee that the Firm has agreed to represent you. Do not send confidential or urgent legal information until the Firm has confirmed an appropriate secure communication channel and engagement.

10. Children

This website is intended for adults seeking legal information or services. Do not knowingly submit a child’s personal information unless you are legally authorised to do so and it is necessary for the enquiry.

11. Changes and contact

The Firm may update this notice as its services, technology or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page. Questions or requests may be sent to aibangbeelaw@gmail.com or by telephone on 0802 329 8459.
